Dirt Rich – Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

EnglishNorsk
In short: Dirt Rich has no user accounts, and we do not collect your name or email through the game. Your game progress is stored only on your device. To show the rewarded ads you choose to watch, the game uses Google AdMob, and to learn how the game is used it collects usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase. Both process some device and usage data as described below. You can change your ad choices under GEAR → Settings → Privacy and ads, and switch the statistics off under GEAR → Settings → Usage statistics.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Ocean Eiendom AS, organisation number 919 698 411, Norway. Contact: oceandevelopnor@gmail.com.

3. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Contract – running the game (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Consent – personalised ads, the use of your advertising ID where consent is required, and usage statistics in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw consent at any time under GEAR → Settings → Privacy and ads. Reminders are sent only if you turn them on.
  • Legitimate interests – non-personalised ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention, and usage statistics outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object to the statistics by switching them off under GEAR → Settings → Usage statistics.

4. Ads, statistics and your choices

The game shows only rewarded ads that you start yourself, for example to double your earnings after an expedition. There are no forced ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. On iPhone and iPad the game first asks, with Apple's own question, whether it may use your device's advertising identifier for tracking; if you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, it is not used and the ads are not personalised. You can change your choice under GEAR → Settings → Privacy and ads, reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings, and allow or deny tracking on iPhone and iPad under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Read more in how Google uses data in ads.

Usage statistics follow the same consent form where it is shown, and are off until you have answered it. Wherever you live, you can switch them off under GEAR → Settings → Usage statistics. Reminders can be switched on and off under GEAR → Settings → Reminders, or in your phone's notification settings. Read more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Service providers and transfers outside the EEA

  • Google (AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, Google Play) – Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple (the App Store, on iPhone and iPad) – Apple Privacy Policy

Google may process data in the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

6. How long data is kept

  • Game progress stays on your device until you delete the app or its data.
  • Ad data is kept by Google according to Google's policies.
  • Usage statistics that are tied to an identifier are kept by Google for up to 14 months after you last played. After that only aggregated figures remain.
  • Emails to us are deleted when your request is resolved, unless we must keep them longer.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because we have no accounts, your game data sits only on your device, and deleting the app removes it. For questions about any of your rights, contact us at oceandevelopnor@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet, or to the authority where you live.

8. Children

Dirt Rich is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data sent between the game and Google is encrypted in transit. Game progress on your device is protected by the app storage of Android or iOS.

10. Changes

We will update this policy when the game or the law changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

3. Rettslig grunnlag (personvernforordningen)

  • Avtale – drift av spillet (art. 6 nr. 1 b).
  • Samtykke – tilpasset reklame, bruk av reklame-ID der samtykke kreves, og bruksstatistikk i EØS, Storbritannia og Sveits (art. 6 nr. 1 a). Du kan trekke samtykket når som helst under GEAR → Settings → Privacy and ads. Påminnelser sendes bare hvis du slår dem på.
  • Berettiget interesse – ikke-tilpasset reklame, måling av reklame og hindring av svindel, og bruksstatistikk utenfor EØS, Storbritannia og Sveits (art. 6 nr. 1 f). Du kan protestere mot statistikken ved å slå den av under GEAR → Settings → Usage statistics.

4. Reklame, statistikk og dine valg

Spillet viser bare belønningsreklame som du selv starter, for eksempel for å doble fortjenesten etter en ekspedisjon. Det er ingen tvungen reklame. I EØS, Storbritannia og Sveits blir du spurt om samtykke i Googles samtykkeskjema før tilpasset reklame vises. På iPhone og iPad spør spillet først, med Apples eget spørsmål, om det kan bruke enhetens reklame-ID til sporing; velger du «Be appen om ikke å spore», brukes den ikke, og reklamen blir ikke tilpasset. Du kan endre valget under GEAR → Settings → Privacy and ads, tilbakestille eller slette reklame-ID-en i innstillingene på Android, og tillate eller avslå sporing på iPhone og iPad under Innstillinger → Personvern og sikkerhet → Sporing. Les mer om hvordan Google bruker data i reklame.

Bruksstatistikken følger det samme samtykkeskjemaet der det vises, og er avslått til du har svart på det. Uansett hvor du bor, kan du slå den av under GEAR → Settings → Usage statistics. Påminnelser slår du av og på under GEAR → Settings → Reminders, eller i varslingsinnstillingene på telefonen. Les mer om hvordan Google bruker informasjon fra apper som bruker tjenestene deres.
